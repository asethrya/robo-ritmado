# Sprites de Brinquedolândia — Robô Ritmado 0.4

Arquivo final: `pixel-brinquedos-danca-v4.png`, PNG com canal alfa, 1536 × 1024 pixels. Preparado pela ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, em duas chamadas; sem API/CLI de geração. Os sprites são novas interpretações das referências da equipe, mantendo a identidade de Pixel e o traje de brinquedos. O desenho pode receber refinamentos posteriores.

Referências: `Outfit - Brinquedolándia.png` para identidade, roupa e guitarra; o atlas anterior `pixel-doces-danca-v3.png` apenas para câmera e variedade das poses. O primeiro resultado serviu como alvo da extração de fundo. Os originais em E:/Mozi/Jogo - WEB-01 foram preservados.

O jogo utiliza limites por pose, escala consistente e fundo transparente. As cores RGB do PNG podem ainda conter informações do fundo onde o alfa é zero; o navegador respeita o canal alfa ao compor o personagem.

## Prompt inicial

Use case: stylized-concept / identity-preserving game animation asset. Create a transparent PNG atlas with exactly 8 full-body Pixel robot guitarist dance sprites, 4 columns x 2 rows. Reference image 1 is the EXACT TOY WORLD OUTFIT: white tousled hair, friendly glossy black LED robot face with star eyes, star headphones, red/yellow/blue toy building blocks decorating white layered coat, teddy bear chest charm, multicolor ribbons, star knee/boot armor, toy star guitar made of red/blue/yellow blocks with glowing blue spiral center and little train charm. Retain these unmistakable costume details. Reference image 2 is ONLY the POSE/SCALE/CAMERA layout of the previous candy atlas, do NOT transfer its pink candy costume. Match its polished anime robot game art and full-body three-quarter front camera. Make eight genuinely different poses in sequence: centered strum, lean left strum, heel raised happy, lean right guitar raised, crouch strum, side-step, joyful one-knee-up hop, returning groove. Same character proportions and costume in every pose. Each sprite fully enclosed INSIDE its own equal cell with GENEROUS EMPTY transparent gutters, minimum 10 percent cell padding on ALL sides. Same foot baseline and scale across cells; all hair guitar cape and boots must fit. No overlap between cells. No scenery, floor, cast shadows, labels, grids, borders, extra objects or text. Output actual transparent alpha, not a drawn checkerboard. Suggested 1536x1024 landscape atlas. Focus on clearly different red-blue-yellow TOY outfit, consistent character identity, leg/arm pose changes and fully separated sprites.
