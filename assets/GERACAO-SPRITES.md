# Origem dos sprites de Pixel — 0.3

Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada (`image_gen`), com transparência solicitada em ambas as chamadas. Nenhuma geração por API/CLI foi usada. A animação é composta em JavaScript, sem alterar os arquivos originais da equipe.

Referências: `Outfit - Reino dos Doces.png` para identidade, roupa e guitarra; `Pixel Poses.png` para personalidade e exemplos de ações. O primeiro resultado gerado serviu como alvo da segunda chamada, que ajustou a composição. O atlas final foi copiado para `pixel-doces-danca-v3.png` e os limites das poses foram registrados no código para manter escala e apoio dos pés consistentes.

## Prompt inicial

Use case: stylized-concept, identity-preserving game animation asset. Create ONE production sprite atlas of Pixel, the robot guitarist in the supplied references, on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Image 1 is the exact CANDY WORLD COSTUME and character identity reference: retain the white tousled hair, glossy black friendly LED face with pink/cyan eyes, candy swirl headphones, white pink candy crystal coat and boots, and ornate pink candy guitar with spiral center. Image 2 is ONLY an action/personality reference, do not use its dark outfit or its layout. Output a STRICT uniform grid of FOUR columns and TWO rows, EIGHT complete full-body character sprites, no panels, borders, labels, text, sheet decorations, environment, floor, backdrop or cast shadows. Desired canvas 2048x1536, eight equal 512x768 cells. Same camera three-quarter front facing slightly toward viewer's left, same character proportions, same scale, same central x anchor, feet baseline at 92 percent of each cell, and at least 7 percent transparent padding inside EVERY cell. Entire body including head/hair/headphones/boots/guitar must fit in each cell, no clipping or overlapping neighboring cells. Sequential rhythmic guitar-dance poses left to right, top to bottom: 1 neutral springy bent knees strumming, 2 lean left with guitar downstroke and left knee bend, 3 straighten with right heel lifted and smiling eyes, 4 lean right with upstroke and right knee bend, 5 crouch slightly both knees with guitar angled upward, 6 step left with raised left heel and playful visor, 7 joyful small hop with one knee lifted while holding guitar, 8 return toward centered groove with guitar strum. These must be meaningfully DIFFERENT body/arm/leg poses suitable to animate as a loop, NOT eight copies merely translated. Keep guitar on strap and hands plausible in all poses. Crisp polished anime fantasy robot game art faithful to reference, white pearl materials, pink cyan iridescence, candy motifs, friendly for children. Background must be actual transparent alpha, not gray/white/checkerboard drawn into image. One single atlas, exactly eight sprites.

## Prompt de ajuste, usado para o arquivo final

Edit target: supplied Pixel candy-costume dance sprite atlas. KEEP the exact character identity, clothing, guitar, colors and eight poses. Correct ONLY the layout and framing. Create a precise 4-column x 2-row atlas, exactly 8 sprites. Each sprite MUST be reduced to fit INSIDE its own equal cell with a FULL EMPTY TRANSPARENT GUTTER on all four sides. Each cell should have its sprite centered horizontally, feet baseline 85% down its cell, character maximum height 72% of cell, entire hair guitar cape and boots completely enclosed. All eight cells same scale. Never touch cell borders or sheet borders; never overlap another cell. In particular recover the entire guitar tip on the top right pose and cape tips on the left poses. Transparent padding is essential for runtime animation cropping. NO added decorations, panels, boxes, labels, shadows, ground or environment. Preserve genuine alpha transparency. This must be a coherent usable 8-frame 2D animation atlas with completely separate sprites and consistent framing, not a collage.

## Resultado efetivo

O gerador retornou um PNG de **1448 × 1086**, com canal alfa, em vez da resolução sugerida no prompt. Por isso o jogo usa retângulos medidos para cada pose em vez de supor células de 512 × 768. O fundo foi verificado por leitura de pixels. As poses são novas interpretações das referências e podem receber refinamentos de desenho nas próximas revisões.
